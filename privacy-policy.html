AccountVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Who we are

AccountVault (the “App”) is a mobile app that helps you keep a record of the online accounts you have created. It is operated by Vinks, an independent developer based in Nigeria (“we”, “us”). Contact: achikehr@gmail.com.

AccountVault is not a password manager. It never asks for, stores or shares the passwords of the accounts you track. Please do not type passwords into the notes field.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your email address and the password you choose for your AccountVault login. Your password is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • The account entries you save: app or website name, website address, the email you used, login method (for example Google or email), category, notes, and the dates you created or last used the entry.

Information collected through our service providers

  • Subscription information: whether you have a Pro subscription, purchase and renewal details, and your AccountVault user ID, processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.
  • Technical information such as IP address and device or operating system details, which our providers process to deliver and secure the service.

The App does not contain advertising, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not collect your location, contacts or photos.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To store your account entries and keep them in sync across your devices.
  • To provide Pro features and manage your subscription.
  • To send emails that are necessary for your account, such as email confirmation and password reset. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To respond to support and privacy requests, and to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with providers that process it for us:

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting, where your email and account entries are stored.
  • RevenueCat: subscription and entitlement management.
  • Google Play: app distribution and billing.
  • Resend: delivery of account emails, which requires your email address.
  • Expo: app build and delivery tooling.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or when necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Data stored on your device

The App keeps a copy of your account entries in a local database on your phone so it works offline. Changes made offline are uploaded when you reconnect. Signing out does not remove this copy; deleting your account (below) or uninstalling the App does.

Retention and account deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the App: sign in, open Settings and tap Delete My Account. This permanently deletes your account entries and your sign-in record from our database and removes the copy on your device.

If you cannot access the App, email achikehr@gmail.com from the address you registered with and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

After deletion, copies may remain in our providers’ backups or logs for a limited period until they are overwritten. Purchase records held by Google and RevenueCat are kept under their own policies and as required by tax and financial law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of your data. This may include rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, the GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA. Email us to exercise any of them; we will respond within the time required by law. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our providers may store and process data in countries other than your own. By using the App you understand that your information may be transferred internationally. We choose providers that apply recognised safeguards for such transfers.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are stored hashed, and database rules restrict each user to their own entries. No system is perfectly secure, and your account entries are not end-to-end encrypted. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you as required by law.

Children

AccountVault is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed, and we will tell you about material changes in the App or by email.
